# NeuroBrew release notes

Newest first. Each release is one tested build; the time is when the release was cut (UTC). Earlier builds were not recorded here.

## Release 28: Look-up reads real listings

2026-10-06 19:14:11 UTC

- Look up this coffee now reads listings that are written as paragraphs, not just ones with labelled lines: origin and region, varieties, process and elevation are picked out of the text.
- A spelling difference between your coffee name and the shop's (such as Chelbesa and Chelbessa) no longer stops the match, and a process word at the end of the title (Washed, Natural) is read.
- Values taken from a paragraph are marked so you check them before keeping them.

## Release 27: Look up a coffee from the roaster's own shop

2026-10-06 18:03:20 UTC

- New "Look up this coffee" button under the coffee name and roaster. It reads the roaster's own online shop listing (shops built on Shopify) and offers the origin, process, varietal, elevation, roast level and tasting notes the listing states. You check every line before anything fills in.
- Type the roaster's website once, or paste a link to the coffee's own page. NeuroBrew remembers the website for that roaster in this browser, and never replaces something you already entered unless you tick it.
- Your browser asks the roaster's website directly. Nothing goes through NeuroBrew, and nothing about your cuppings is sent. About NeuroBrew and the guide explain this.

## Release 26: A tidier menu, a user guide and release notes

2026-10-06 16:31:21 UTC

- The Menu button now sits in the same place at the top right of every screen, including the first one. On wide screens it opens as a panel under the header.
- New "How to use NeuroBrew" guide in the menu: the six steps, what each screen does, and how your history helps. Tap a section to open it.
- "About NeuroBrew" is rewritten to say what the app is, what it is not, where its guidance comes from and what to keep in mind.
- The bottom of the menu now shows which release you are on, with the date and time it was made, and a Release notes page lists what changed in each release.

## Release 25: Dial it in knows about new bags

2026-10-06 (time not recorded)

- A new bag of a coffee you have already brewed is recognised from its roast date, and your dialed-in recipe carries over to it.
- The trail marks cups from a new bag, and does not count a change made on a new bag as something you tried, because the bag is mixed in.
- A first brew of a coffee can start from where your other coffees ended up, when at least three of them agree. It is only a suggestion, and you choose whether to use it.

## Release 24: Dial it in remembers and lets you lock a recipe

2026-10-06 (time not recorded)

- Dial it in remembers what it suggested and what you tasted, and compares each try with the cup it started from.
- A change that already scored lower is not suggested again; it offers a different change instead.
- Lock in a cup as your recipe for a coffee and method. New brews start from it, and you can unlock it any time.
- Notices when the problem looks like the coffee (stale or off-tasting words, an old bag) rather than the recipe, and when several changes in a row have not helped.

## Release 23: Dial it in is now a workflow

2026-10-06 (time not recorded)

- Dial it in shows every try of a coffee on a method, in order, with what changed and whether it helped.
- It starts from what you already cupped: your scores point to what to change next, and the next brew starts from your best try.
- Open it from the start screen, your history, a saved cup or the recipe screen. The start screen and history list the coffees you are still dialing in.
